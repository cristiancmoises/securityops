Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-06 • Version 5.0.0 • GPL-3.0

TL;DR

Security Ops collects nothing. No telemetry. No analytics. No accounts. No "anonymized usage data." All settings stay in your browser's local storage. The only network requests are to fetch public blocklists from a CDN. That's it.

1. What data we collect

None. The extension runs entirely on your device. We do not have a server. We do not receive logs. We do not receive crash reports.

2. What data the extension stores locally

The extension uses your browser's storage.sync and storage.local APIs to remember the following on your device only:

  • Settings: which categories you've enabled (ads, trackers, etc.), your accent color, dark theme preference.
  • Whitelist: domains you've manually whitelisted.
  • Proxy config: host/port/scheme if you've enabled the proxy.
  • Block logs: URLs of blocked requests and a category, capped at 500 entries (oldest discarded).
  • Blocklist cache: cached copies of public blocklists, refreshed every 24 hours.

If you sign into your browser and have sync enabled, your settings and whitelist sync across your devices via your browser vendor (Google/Mozilla/Microsoft). The logs and blocklist cache stay on each device.

3. Network requests the extension makes

The extension makes these external network requests:

  • Blocklist downloads from cdn.jsdelivr.net (delivering the Hagezi DNS blocklists). Refreshed once per 24 hours via the alarms API.
  • IP lookup to api.ipify.org, only when you explicitly enable "IP Lookup" and click "Show IP". Disabled by default.

No request is ever sent to any server controlled by the developer. You can verify this by inspecting the source: every fetch() call is to one of those two endpoints.

4. Permissions explained

  • storage — to remember your settings.
  • declarativeNetRequest — to block ad/tracker/malware requests.
  • declarativeNetRequestFeedback — to populate the blocked-request log (Chrome unpacked-mode only).
  • proxy — to route traffic through your local Tor / proxy when you enable it.
  • tabs, activeTab — to broadcast theme updates to open tabs.
  • browsingData — for the Panic button (clears cookies/cache/history on demand).
  • webNavigation — to detect blocked navigations for the log.
  • alarms — to schedule the 24-hour blocklist refresh.
  • host_permissions: <all_urls> — required for content scripts to apply the dark theme on every site, and for the proxy to handle every request.

5. Your data is yours

You can clear all stored data at any time:

  • "Clear" button in the popup → clears stats & logs.
  • "Panic" button → clears cookies, history, cache, localStorage, IndexedDB, formData, AND closes all tabs.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its data immediately.
